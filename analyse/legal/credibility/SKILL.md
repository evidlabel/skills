# credibility

Same questions for every party, on a statement and a topic. No score, rank, or label for the person. Demeanour and tone are not a factor. Name the passage.

A contradiction is two statements in one document, a statement here and one made elsewhere, or a statement against a fact. One of two contradictory statements is false. Showing that is usually cheaper than finding a contradicting fact.

A history of false or misleading statements lowers a later statement. A recorded condition that includes psychosis or lying bears on the statement.

A story that depends on a rare event needs stronger evidence.

Corroboration from other witnesses weighs more than a single source. Significant contact beforehand, and statements that match in the details, can mean the testimony was coordinated.
