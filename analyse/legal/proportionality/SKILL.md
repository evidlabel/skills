# analysis-proportionality

- list the measures first
- for each measure: suitability, necessity, balance, whether the stated reasons are in the record, whether parents and child took part
- evidence for, evidence against, and gaps
- what the law requires comes from the statute criteria
- no score and no conclusion on the measure
