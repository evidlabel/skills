# determining field  
Law is a broad field, in family and social cases, aspects may touch different subfields of law. 
Example, a family law case may involve civil matters (financial settlements), criminal matters (like findings of abuse), 
human rights related matters (right to family life) and social law matters (care placements). 

The way to deal with this is: 
- Be aware of angles from all (potentially) relevant fields of law. 
- Get the relevant laws and guidelines. 
- Use memos/briefs to inform the user of perspectives from areas of law other than the one they started with.
