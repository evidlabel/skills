
- LAF are linguistic constructs designed to achieve a reduced chance of accountablity (primarily for making false statements)
- they can be used by litigants, professionals, or even written into law itself, whereby the law is designed to achieve a low degree of accountability
  - example: "The professional may, based on a professional evaluation of the situation, do X" 
    --> this is a LAF, given that the professional by definition makes a "professional" evaluation, therefore can at any point implement X.
