# credibility

Same questions for every party, on a statement and a topic. No score, rank, or label for the person. Demeanour and tone are not a factor. Name the passage.

What a contradiction, a history of false or misleading statements, a recorded condition, a rare event, or corroboration means for the statement comes from the statute, the judgments, and the guidance in the evid set, for this jurisdiction and the date of the statement. Read those.

A contradiction is two statements in one document, a statement here and one made elsewhere, or a statement against a fact. `language` labels the spans. Showing the conflict is usually cheaper than finding a contradicting fact.

A recorded condition is taken from the record. Whether it bears on the statement comes from the standard just read.

Corroboration is other witnesses, contact between them beforehand, and statements that match in the details.
