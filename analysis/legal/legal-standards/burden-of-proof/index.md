# burden-of-proof

Check the type of court and the burden of proof that applies.

The burden depends on the matter.

- Family law uses the civil burden.
- A false statement to the court uses the criminal burden.

Confirm both for this jurisdiction and the date.
The figures used in a briefing are in `analysis/legal/probability`.
