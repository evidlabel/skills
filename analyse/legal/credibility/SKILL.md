# analysis-credibility

- same factors for every party, per statement and topic
- consistency within an account, consistency with the record, stated conduct against recorded conduct, how well a professional or third-party statement is grounded
- no score, rank, or label for a person
- demeanour and tone are not factors
- a factor row cites its source
